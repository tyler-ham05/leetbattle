# Match result screen and light mode fixes

## Description
Serves US-5 (UI/UX, #74). When a match ends, the game page shows a full-width Victory!/Defeat screen that makes the winner clear to everyone. The light/dark button moves to the top bar of every page, and light mode is fixed on the practice editor and lobby.

## Specs
- When a match ends, the game page shows "Victory!" or "Defeat", then "<Team> wins" in that team's color.
- Each half of the screen shows a team, its score and its players. The winners get crowns in the winning team's color.
- Scores count up from 0. Victory slams in and Defeat sinks in, and the frame comes in together with the halves.
- The result screen has Play again (goes to /lobby) and Home buttons.
- The old `/results` page is removed.
- The light/dark button is in the top bar of every page, including the landing page and the signed-in home, and is no longer in settings.
- Home shows just the player's name above the title.
- Light mode: the practice editor doesn't crash on short hex colors, and the lobby VS bars and open seats are visible.

## Requirements
- No new dependencies.
- Use only the theme classes defined in docs/frontend/theming.md.
- Frontend tests (`frontend/test/`) do not touch backend services.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A finished match shows Victory! or Defeat with the winning team's name in its color.
2. Both teams' players and scores are shown, and the winners have crowns.
3. Play again goes to /lobby and Home goes to /.
4. /results no longer exists.
5. Every page has the light/dark button in its top bar.
6. In light mode, the practice editor loads and the lobby VS bars and open seats are visible.
