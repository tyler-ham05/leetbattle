# Ladder: pick a queue, Play, and a match-start screen with the players' names

## Description
Serves US-10 (Polished Match Experience, #177). The Ladder becomes one standard launcher: pick a team size and a gamemode, see your rank card, press Play. While you wait, your team and the rivals slide in at the sides and the seats fill as players join. When the match is found, a match-start screen shows every player's name, a VS that spins in and is sliced, and then the game opens. This replaces the arena cards and the 2-1 countdown from US-10-T-9. QueueService now sends both teams' names with the match.

## Specs

### Ladder (`app/ladder/page.tsx`)
- **Layout:** a centered column: the "Ladder" heading, two dropdown tiles side by side, the rank card, the queue count, and the Play button. The sides stay empty until the player queues.
- **Team size tile:** a dropdown with 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 (2v2 to start). The tile shows the choice in large mono type. A new choice rolls in vertically instead of swapping.
- **Gamemode tile:** a dropdown with Classic as its only option for now. More modes are added as options later.
- **Rank card:** a "RANK" label, an empty badge box and an empty progress bar. It is blank on purpose until ranks exist.
- **Background:** the chosen team size in very large, very faint mono type behind the column, centered on the "v". It fades out evenly toward the middle, with a fine noise mask so the gradient shows no banding. A new size fades and sharpens in, and its digits slide outward from the "v". There is no grid.
- **Play:** a full-width "PLAY" button that sends `queue:join` with the chosen team size. It is disabled while not connected.
- **Queued:**
  - The button becomes "WAITING" with a timer counting up from 0:00 in the same type as "WAITING", and a light running around its edge. The light moves evenly along the edges and eases slightly at the corners.
  - A square "Leave" button sits to its right and sends `queue:leave`.
  - Both dropdowns are disabled.
  - The number of players waiting in that queue shows above the button as "N IN QUEUE".
  - The background dims a little, while the dropdowns, rank card, timer and queue count stay readable.
- **Team columns:** hidden until the player queues. On joining, "YOUR TEAM" and "RIVALS" glide in toward the center beside the rank card and grow to full size, each a column of large seat cards for the chosen size. The headings use the page's small muted label style.
  - **Seats:** they fill in the order the match is made, using the queue's waiting count: the player's own seat first, then the rest of their team, then the rivals. There are no player icons yet, so seats show names:
    - The player's own seat shows their name and a "YOU" tag.
    - Other filled seats show "Player found" until the match sends the real names, then show the names in the team's color.
    - An open seat reads "Searching", with dots that count up ("." then ".." then "...") and start again. Open seats have a lighter solid fill and no border, so they read as empty.
  - **No dotted or dashed lines:** not on seats, the rank badge or anywhere else on the page.
  - **Count:** each column shows how many of its seats are filled ("1 / 2").
  - **Leaving:** the columns slide back out when the player leaves the queue or the game opens.
- **Match found:** the button shows "MATCH FOUND" with a short nod, and the match-start screen plays.
- **Copy:** no trailing periods. The QueueService error alert stays as it is.

### Match-start screen (new component under `app/ladder/`)
- **Covers the page:** it lasts about 2.7 seconds, then opens `/game/<gameId>`. GameService starts the match 5 seconds after creating it, so there is time to spare.
- **Two rows of names, in capitals:** the player's team in `--lb-primary` above the middle, the rivals in `--lb-secondary` below it.
- **Names:**
  - The player's own name has a small "YOU" tag.
  - Names in a row are separated by a thin vertical bar (no dots).
  - Faded copies of the row sit on either side.
- **Sized to fit:** one font size for both rows, set so the longest row fits the screen width. Long usernames in a 3v3 shrink instead of being cut off.
- **Motion:** the rows sweep in from opposite sides, drift slowly past each other and sweep out.
- **VS:** plain text with no circle. It spins in once and settles. A blade then cuts it corner to corner, and the two halves slide apart along the cut. It is one whole VS until the moment of the cut, so no seam shows before it.
- **Missing names:** if `queue:matched` has no names, the rows read "YOUR TEAM" and "RIVALS".

### Lobby (`app/lobby/page.tsx`)
- **Seats match the Ladder:** player rows, the join or switch button and open seats are the same height (72px), corner radius and padding as the Ladder seats.
- **Open seats:** a lighter solid fill, dimmed, with no dashed outline.
- **No "You" tag:** the player's own row shows no "You" label. The edit name button stays.

### QueueService (`service/QueueService/src/server.ts`)
- `queue:matched` also sends `yourTeam` and `rivals`: the display names of the receiving player's team and the other team, in queue order. The team split is the same one sent to GameService.
- `gameId` is unchanged, so anything reading only `gameId` keeps working.
- The frontend `QueueMatch` type (`app/ladder/socket-context.tsx`) gains the two optional lists.

### Motion
- **Where:** new animations live in the MOTION section of `app/globals.css` and move with `transform`, `opacity` and `filter` only. The one exception is the seat column height while the team size changes.
- **Reduced motion:** each animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` override. Columns and the background number simply fade, the "Searching" dots and the WAITING light stay still, the rows don't sweep, the VS shows whole and then fades, and there is no blade.
- **Docs:** `docs/frontend/animation.md` loses the Ladder match reveal row (arena cards) and gains rows for the team size background, the queue and the match-start screen.

## Requirements
- No new dependencies.
- Use only the theme classes defined in `docs/frontend/theming.md`. No decorative dots and no circles around VS.
- Frontend tests (`frontend/test/`) do not touch backend services, and QueueService tests stay in `service/QueueService/test/`. One expect per test.
- Tests that this task changes are listed in the test pseudocode files: the US-9-T-2 and US-10-T-9 Ladder tests, the lobby seat tests, plus any QueueService test of the exact `queue:matched` payload.
- Not in this task: an "in match" player count. It needs GameService to report active games.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The Ladder shows the Team size and Gamemode dropdowns, the blank rank card and one Play button, with no arena cards.
2. Play joins the chosen queue. While waiting, the timer runs, Leave cancels, the dropdowns are locked and the queue count shows.
3. The YOUR TEAM and RIVALS columns appear only while queued, and their seats fill from the queue's waiting count. Filled seats show names or "Player found", and open seats show "Searching" with a lighter fill and no dotted lines.
4. A found match plays the match-start screen with every player's name, the player's own name tagged YOU and names separated by a thin bar, then opens the game.
5. Long names in a 3v3 fit on screen.
6. QueueService sends both teams' names with `queue:matched`, and `gameId` still arrives as before.
7. Every new animation is turned off or calmed under reduced motion.
8. Lobby seats are the Ladder's size, open lobby seats are dimmed with no dashed outline, and the player's own row has no "You" tag.
