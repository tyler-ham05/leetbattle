# Match screens: your team first everywhere, cleaner ban and pick, tidy copy

## Description
Serves US-5 (UI/UX, #74). Players always see their own team first, on the left, in the primary color, from the lobby through the ban and pick screens. The ban and pick screens drop the decorative dots and pills, a ban strikes through as it lands, the clock warns when time is low, and leftover copy and unused code are cleaned up.

## Specs
- Lobby: once a player is seated, their team is on the left in the primary color and called "Your team", and the other team is on the right in the secondary color called "Rival team", even when the player is on team b. Unseated players and onlookers still see Team 1 (left, primary) and Team 2 (right, secondary).
- Game page before play (waiting, banning, picking): the team rosters use the same naming and order (Your team first, primary).
- Ban screen: "Banned by" names are colored by the viewer's side (the viewer's team primary, the other secondary). The Team 1 / Team 2 dot legend is removed, and the progress pips become plain text ("2 of 4 bans made" stays).
- Pick screen: the progress pips are removed in the same way, and the "--" in its copy becomes a proper sentence.
- Topic chips on the ban and pick screens are flat `rounded-md` labels, not pills. The two screens share one question card component, so they don't repeat code.
- When a question is banned, a line strikes across its card (transform-only, with a reduced-motion override).
- Pre-match header: no "0 – 0" score while waiting, banning or picking, only the phase clock.
- Match clock: under 1:00 left, the clock turns `text-danger`.
- The connected-teammate names next to Forfeit are plain muted text, not pills.
- Copy: no period at the end of single lines ("This lobby is full", "Name updated", "Password changed", the match subtitle), errors start with a capital letter, and the home menu's Lobby line no longer says "six-player".
- Unused `home-wheel.tsx` and `home-menu.tsx` are deleted.

## Requirements
- No new dependencies.
- Use only the theme classes defined in docs/frontend/theming.md. No decorative dots or pills.
- Every new animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` override.
- Frontend tests (`frontend/test/`) do not touch backend services.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A team b player sees "Your team" on the left in the primary color in the lobby and on the pre-match game page.
2. The ban and pick screens show no dots, pips or pill chips.
3. A ban strikes through its card as it lands.
4. The pre-match header shows no score.
5. The match clock is red under one minute.
6. The single-line copy has no trailing periods, and the home menu doesn't mention six players.
7. `home-wheel.tsx` and `home-menu.tsx` no longer exist.
