# Busy spinners, Your team / Rival team, match starts after the clash

## Description
Serves US-5 (UI/UX, #74). Anything that waits on a server shows a spinner, so players know it's working. Players see the teams from their own side: "Your team" (always first, in the primary color) and "Rival team". The match starts right after the lobby's Leet vs Battle animation, and the pen-tip wipe no longer cuts off the last letter.

## Specs
- Run and Submit: while one is running, its label swaps for a spinner, it keeps its width and full color, and the other button greys out. Screen readers still hear "Run" / "Submit".
- Form buttons (log in, sign up, send reset link, change password, both settings Save buttons) show a spinner instead of text while pending.
- "Loading question..." in the editor and "Joining match…" on the game page show a spinner beside the text.
- The match top bar and the result screen show the player's own team first as "Your team" in the primary color, and the other team as "Rival team", even when the player was on team b. The result says "Your team wins" or "Rival team wins".
- In the lobby, once seated, the headings read "Your team" / "Rival team" and the other side's open seat says "Switch to rival team". Before joining a team, the lobby still says Team 1 / Team 2.
- The Leet vs Battle clash labels its sides "YOUR TEAM" / "RIVAL TEAM".
- Onlookers (not on a team) still see Team 1 / Team 2.
- The game page plays no loader while banning, picking or playing.
- The pen-tip wipe (sub page loader and landing intro) ends past the wordmark's edges, so the "e" in Battle is not cut off.
- Docs: no decorative dots (theming.md), team names and colors (theming.md), busy states (animation.md).

## Requirements
- No new dependencies.
- Use only the theme classes defined in docs/frontend/theming.md. No decorative dots.
- Every new animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` override.
- Frontend tests (`frontend/test/`) do not touch backend services.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Clicking Run or Submit shows a spinner in that button until the result comes back, and the button does not shrink or fade.
2. Pending log in, sign up, reset and settings buttons show a spinner.
3. On the match top bar and result screen, a player always sees "Your team" first in the primary color.
4. Seated lobby players see "Your team" / "Rival team", and the clash says YOUR TEAM / RIVAL TEAM.
5. After the clash, the ban screen opens with no further loader animation.
6. The LeetBattle wordmark wipe shows the whole last "e".
