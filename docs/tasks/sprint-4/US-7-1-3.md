# Ladder matchmaking queues

## Description
Serves US-7. Players can now invite teammates as a "party" in the Ladder tab. When doing this, a team of players is able to join the same matches together as teammates.

## Specs
- QueueService needs to be updated so that multiple players can enter the queue as a group, and when that group is popped from the queue they are set to be teammates in the game
- On the UI side, there is a button to invite friends that has the exact shape of the leave button when queueing up, however this button should only show if the queue they're joining has a team size is 2 or 3
- When clicked, add a popup for an invite link that users can send to teammates. When the teammate clicks the link, they should see a prompt asking if they want to be added before adding them
- If a teammates is not signed in when they click the link, they must first sign in before they see the prompt. New users should also be able to see the prompt after they click to join and create an account
- The ladder queueing UI also needs to slightly modified so that the circles that represent other players joining a game while waiting in queue should show up by default, having a smooth animation when switching between team sizes
- When a player joins a part, a circle the previous showed a question mark should show that team member joining for all users on the team
- When one users clicks to join the queue the other users cannot click queue button
- Team members in a party cannot join team size queues that are smaller than their current size

## Requirements
- QueueService calls GameService directly and never calls LobbyService.
- Joining a queue, creating a party and accepting an invite all require a signed-in user. The user's identity comes from their auth token, not from the request body or the invite link.
- A party joins and leaves a queue as one unit. When it is taken out of the queue, every member is put on the same team, and the party is never split across two matches.
- A party can only join a queue whose team size is at least the party's size.
- Only one party member can start queueing at a time. Once the party is in a queue, the other members can't press the queue button.
- The invite link must only add the player after they confirm the prompt. A player who isn't signed in is sent to sign in or create an account first, then sees the prompt.
- Every party member sees a teammate fill their question-mark circle when they join the party.
- The invite button has the same shape as the Leave queue button and only shows for the 2v2 and 3v3 queues.
- The player circles show by default and animate smoothly when switching between team sizes.
- If GameService fails to create the match, the players and parties go back to the front of the queue in their original order and see an error.
- New service tests go in `service/QueueService/test/`. Frontend tests don't touch backend services.
- Use only the theme classes in docs/frontend/theming.md.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A player who has picked the 2v2 or 3v3 queue sees an invite button with the same shape as the Leave queue button.
2. A player who has picked the 1v1 queue does not see the invite button.
3. Clicking the invite button opens a popup with an invite link.
4. A signed-in player who opens the invite link sees a prompt asking if they want to join the party, and is only added after they confirm.
5. A player who is not signed in and opens the invite link is asked to sign in first, then sees the prompt.
6. A new user who opens the invite link can create an account, then sees the prompt.
7. When a player joins the party, a question-mark circle changes to show that player for every member of the party.
8. The player circles show by default on the Ladder page and animate smoothly when switching between team sizes.
9. When one party member clicks to join a queue, the whole party is queued and the other members can't click the queue button.
10. A party of 2 in the 2v2 queue is put on the same team in the created game.
11. A party of 3 in the 3v3 queue is put on the same team in the created game.
12. A party of 2 in the 3v3 queue is put on the same team, with one more player from the queue filling the team.
13. A party of 3 can't join the 2v2 or 1v1 queue, and a party of 2 can't join the 1v1 queue.
14. A party is never split across two matches, even when many players join the same queue at once.
15. LobbyService is never called when a ladder match starts.
16. If GameService fails, the players and parties stay queued in their original order and see an error.
