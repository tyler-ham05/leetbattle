# Landing page polish

## Description
Serves US-5 (UI/UX). A polish pass on the landing page from US-5-T-14 and US-5-T-15, so a first-time visitor gets to the point faster and the page works on a phone. Design: "E · Polish" on the landing page canvas.

## Specs
- No full-screen intro on the landing page: the LeetBattle wordmark draws in right away.
- The hero has one "Play" link instead of "Find a match" and "Private lobby". "Your move" also has one "Play" link. The nav keeps its sign-in link.
- The match demo plays once and stays on its finished state ("Team 1 wins"). A "Replay" button under it plays it again from the start.
- On a narrow screen the two team panels of the demo stack, and the clock is smaller.
- "Two teams" sits on the left of the page and "Same problems" on the right.

## Requirements
- The changes are in `frontend/app/_landing/` (`landing-page.tsx`, `match-demo.tsx`, `scroll-sections.tsx`) and `frontend/app/globals.css`.
- Animations stay CSS only. No new dependencies.
- Use only the theme classes defined in docs/frontend/theming.md.
- Frontend tests (`frontend/test/`) do not touch backend services.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The landing page shows no intro overlay.
2. The hero and "Your move" each have a single "Play" link, and no "Find a match" or "Private lobby" link.
3. The demo does not loop, and a "Replay" button restarts it.
4. The demo's team panels stack on a narrow screen.
5. "Two teams" is aligned left and "Same problems" right.
